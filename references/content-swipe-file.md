## What to steal for LinkedIn

- **Hooks that repeat and win:** "$10k grandma loan → $235M", "two teens + bike → global", "quit at 18 → $3M/year".
- **Format sweet spot:** first line = big number or transformation, last line = tactical takeaway — maps directly onto this skill's hook/re-hook and actionable-advice beats.
- **Whitespace accounts** (real story, low reel volume): @joshandbrent and @hellonecole — good pattern to imitate when the user has a niche story nobody else is telling yet.
